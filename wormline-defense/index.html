HOW TO PLAY
A giant worm descends a winding route toward your core. Your organism fires automatically.

• Tap a segment to focus all fire on it.
• Tap empty space to return to auto-targeting.
• Press & hold a segment to inspect it.

Sealed mutation capsules hide upgrades — crack one to 50% to reveal its category, destroy it to claim a reward. Kill the head to drop the whole worm fast, or farm its body for power. Anything that reaches the core hurts — every surviving segment adds damage.
