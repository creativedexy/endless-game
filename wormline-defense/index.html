HOW TO PLAY
A colossal worm descends a shifting route toward the breach. Your organism fires on its own — drag left and right (or press ← →) to slide your turret and line up with what you want to hit.

• Weapons fire straight up (or down, for airborne ones) from wherever they are — there's no auto-aim, you have to get under your target.
• The readout above your turret shows whatever's locked in your lane right now.
• Sealed mutation capsules hide rewards from two separate threads: Abilities (graft new weapons like the Spore Wing, Husk Roller or Cinder Gland, then level and evolve them) and Boosts (short, practical stat upgrades — extra shots, more damage, faster fire, tougher core). Crack a capsule to 50% to reveal its category, destroy it to claim a reward.

Kill the head to collapse the whole worm fast, or farm its body for power. Anything that reaches the breach hurts — every surviving segment adds damage. Everything you draft lasts for the whole run and is lost only when the run ends.
